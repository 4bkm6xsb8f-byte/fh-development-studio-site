Process

Three steps, from the first conversation to the app in people's hands.

01

Plan the Whole System

We map out who uses it, what the app does, and what has to sit behind it: the server, the dashboard, the payments. Then we agree on what goes in the first working version.

02

Design and Build It Together

The app, the backend, and the admin screens get built side by side, so they fit when they meet. You see working pieces early, not a finished surprise.

03

Launch and Keep It Running

We handle app store submission, fix what real use turns up, and keep improving it after launch.
