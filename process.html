Process

How we build the app and everything behind it.

An app that works needs a server, data, and often a dashboard or a way to pay. We plan those together from the first conversation, so nothing gets bolted on late and nothing gets built that you won't use.

Overview

Plan the whole thing, build it in step, and keep it running.

It works the same whether the project is one iPhone app or a customer app, a staff dashboard, and a payment system.

01

Plan the Whole System

Who uses it, what each person needs to do, which platforms, and what has to sit behind the app. Then the smallest version worth launching.

02

Build It Together

Screens, server, and dashboards are built side by side, with tests, so the pieces fit and stay easy to change.

03

Launch and Keep It Running

App store submission, support pages, fixes, and steady improvement once people are using it.

Detail

What each stage covers, from the first conversation to after launch.

Planning and What's Included

We work out what needs to be built, what can wait, and where the hard parts are. That covers the whole system: the app, the data it needs, who manages that data, and whether money changes hands.

Design for Every Screen

We design the customer app and the staff or owner screens together, so each person gets a layout that fits their job.

Building the App and the Backend

The app, server, and database are built in step, with automated tests where a mistake would be costly. Choices about the tools it's built with favor what will be easy to update later.

Testing and Launch

Before launch we test on real devices, check what happens with no signal or a failed payment, prepare privacy and support pages, and handle app store review.

After Launch

Once people are using it, we fix what comes up, keep things backed up and updated, and plan the next round of improvements from real use.
