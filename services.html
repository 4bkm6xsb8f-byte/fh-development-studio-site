Backends, APIs, and Admin Dashboards

The server and database that hold your data, and the screens your staff use to manage it. We set it up so each person sees only what their role needs. Midwest Operations gives the office a full web dashboard and the field crew a phone app, both on one shared API.

Payments, Subscriptions, and Owner Portals
