Staff SMS Alerts

How FH Development Studio staff opt in to text alerts.

This page is for the small group of people who run FH Development Studio. It explains what the texts are, how a staff member agrees to receive them, and how to stop them at any time.

What These Messages Are

Alerts about work waiting in the admin workspace.

Who receives them

Only the owners and staff of FH Development Studio who hold an account in the internal admin workspace at app.fhdevelopmentstudio.com. Clients, app users, and people who contact us through the website never receive a text from this number. There is no marketing list, and there is nothing to sign up for here unless you work with us.

What the messages say

A short alert that something needs attention: a new inquiry or support request has arrived, or a job or backup needs looking at. A message looks like this: "FH Development Studio: 2 new inquiries waiting for review. Reply STOP to opt out." Nothing promotional is sent to this number, and no customer information is included in the text itself.

Which number they come from

Texts are sent from +1 (888) 700-0964. Message frequency varies with how much work comes in. Message and data rates may apply.

How to stop them

Reply STOP to any message to opt out, and HELP to get help. You can also email support@fhdevelopmentstudio.com and ask to be removed. Opting out takes effect immediately, and it does not affect anything else about your account.

What happens to your number

The mobile number you give here is used only to send these staff alerts. It is stored in the FH Development Studio admin workspace, it is never sold, rented, or shared with anyone else, and it is not used for marketing. See the Privacy Policy for how we handle information generally.

Opt In

Agree to receive staff text alerts.

Fill this in yourself, with your own mobile number. Nobody can opt someone else in, and alerts are off until you agree here.

Submitting this records your agreement, the wording you agreed to, and the date and time, in the FH Development Studio admin workspace. You can withdraw it at any time by replying STOP to a message or emailing support@fhdevelopmentstudio.com.
