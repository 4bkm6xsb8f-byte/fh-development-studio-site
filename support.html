For Product Support

If you are reaching out about an app issue, include the app name, device model, operating system version, what you expected to happen, and the specific behavior you saw instead.

  • Helpful details include screenshots, timestamps, steps to reproduce the issue, and whether the problem happens consistently.
  • If the issue involves notifications or network checks, note whether settings, Focus modes, or connectivity may be affecting behavior.

For New Project Inquiries

If you're getting in touch about a new app or system, a short summary of your goals is the best place to start.

  • Share what you want to build, who it's for, and whether it needs things like staff dashboards, payments, or offline use.
  • Include timing, target platforms, and any launch constraints if those are already known so the early recommendation can be more useful.

Types of Requests We Can Help With

We can help with apps for iPhone, Android, Mac, and the web, the backends and dashboards behind them, payments and owner portals, and improvements to software you already have.

  • FHWebCheck: monitoring questions, setup clarification, status interpretation, and usability feedback.
  • FHCams: camera management questions, property organization, interface feedback, feature requests, and live-view troubleshooting.
  • FHNetworkMap: scan results, missing devices, alert settings, and device editing.
  • New apps and systems: planning what's included, backends and dashboards, payments, integrations, performance problems, and support after launch.

Suggested Inquiry Format

  • Support: app or site name, device or browser, issue summary, expected behavior, and screenshots if available.
  • New work: what you need built, who it is for, target platforms, timing, and whether this is a new product or an update to an existing one.

Mydeas

Quick answers about notes, plans, and your account.

Mydeas keeps your notes in step across the devices you use. These are the questions we hear most. For anything about your own account, email support@mydeasapp.com and include the address you signed up with.

Where do I get help, and which devices can I use?

Answers live at mydeasapp.com, and account questions go to support@mydeasapp.com. Mydeas runs on iPhone, on the web, and on Android through Google Play. There is a Mac app as well: download it from mydeasapp.com/downloads/mac. It is signed and notarised by Apple, but it is not in the Mac App Store, so the download comes from the site rather than from Apple.

I can't sign in. What should I check?

You can sign in with an email address and password, with Google, or with Apple. If you first created the account one way, use that same way to get back in. A password reset link works once and expires after an hour, so if it has gone stale, request a new one. On the free plan you can be signed in on three devices at once, and signing in on a fourth signs out the oldest one. You can see and end your own sessions under Settings, in Signed-in devices.

What does the free plan include, and how do I upgrade?

The free plan covers 500 notes, three signed-in devices, 100 MB of attachments, one AI action a day, and 20 images read for text a day. Reaching a limit stops you adding more; it never hides or deletes notes you already have. Paid plans are bought on the web at mydeasapp.com rather than inside the app, so the app only shows which plan your account is on.

I imported from Evernote or Notion. Where did my notes go?

Import lives under Settings, and the Import into box lets you pick the notebook the notes land in. It starts on No notebook. For a Notion or Markdown export, the top-level folders become notebooks unless you choose one yourself. An Evernote .enex file has no folders to read, so those notes arrive unfiled unless you pick a notebook first.

Can I write without a connection?

Yes, on every one of them. On iPhone and Android, edits are stored on the device and merge when you are back online, and you keep searching while offline. The web app works offline too: it keeps a copy of itself on the device, so it opens and stays usable with no connection and merges your changes once there is one. Either way, Mydeas merges changes rather than filing a second conflicting copy for you to find later.

Can I send something to Mydeas from another app?

Yes. Share to Mydeas from any app on your iPhone, and there is a home-screen widget for starting a note without opening the app first. A shared web page becomes a note titled with the page. Shared text puts its first line in the title. Files arrive as attachments. Anything you share while signed out is held and filed once you sign in, so nothing is lost.

How do I see which notes point at the one I'm reading?

A note lists its backlinks: the other notes that link to it. That means you can follow a thread in both directions instead of only forward, without keeping track of the links by hand.

Can I keep a search I run often?

Yes. A search can be saved and reopened later, so a set of terms and filters you use regularly is there to tap rather than something you rebuild each time.

How do reminders on tasks work?

A task can carry a reminder, and it arrives in the app. If you connect your Google Calendar, Mydeas can write the reminder there as well, so it sits alongside the rest of your day. Until you connect a calendar, reminders stay inside Mydeas.

Are the AI features and the text-in-scans search on?

Both are off until you switch them on, under Settings, in AI and scanned text. One exception is worth knowing: accounts created before 24 September 2026 already had reading text in images turned on, because it was working for them before the setting existed. It can be switched off in the same place.

How do I get my notes out, or close my account?

Any note exports as Markdown or HTML, and your whole account exports as one ZIP file, on every plan, up to three full exports an hour. Deleting is under Settings, in Account: you re-type your account email to confirm, and it takes effect straight away with no undo, so export first if you want a copy. One more thing to know: notes are encrypted in transit, but Mydeas is not end-to-end encrypted, because sync, search, and sharing need to read them.

BarCrawl

Quick answers for people out, and for bars.

BarCrawl shows you bars nearby with hours, photos, events, and messages to friends. If you own a bar, you can claim your listing and manage it in the owner portal at portal.gobarcrawl.com. Full help lives at gobarcrawl.com/support.

Who can use BarCrawl?

You need to be at least 21, or the legal drinking age where you are if that is higher. Every account records a date of birth before it can use the app, and signing in with Apple or Google still asks for one, because those services don't share it. An under-age attempt pauses further tries for 24 hours.

How do I plan a crawl, and can I send it to a friend?

Give the crawl a name, pick the bars you want, and put them in the order you plan to visit. You can then open the whole night in your phone's maps app instead of looking each bar up one at a time. A crawl can also be shared, and the friend who opens it saves their own copy, so changes they make don't move your version around.

Why am I seeing game times in the app?

Sports schedules show up in the Feed and in the Events calendar, so you can see which games are on while you are deciding where to go.

I'm in a band or I DJ. Can I post my own dates?

Yes. Bands and DJs claim a profile and post events under their own by-line. It is $19 a month, it has its own portal at entertainer.gobarcrawl.com, and what it includes is written up at gobarcrawl.com/for-entertainers.html.

Signing in, deleting your account, or getting a copy of your data.

You can sign in with an email address and password or with Google, and with Apple on iPhone and iPad. Forgotten passwords reset by email. To delete the account, open Settings, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete Account; it takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. If you are the only owner of a bar with an active subscription, cancel that subscription in the owner portal first. For a copy of your data, email support@gobarcrawl.com and you will get a machine-readable file within 45 days.

Why does it ask for my location, and what is "Remember my area"?

Location is what puts bars near you at the top, and you can also search a different area by hand. Remember my area, in Settings, keeps a coarse area such as your city or metro so local content still works when there is no live location fix. It never stores your exact coordinates, it is off until you turn it on, and turning it off deletes the area already stored.

A bar is missing, closed, or has the wrong details.

Listings are built from Google Maps and Google Places, from OpenStreetMap, and from public government data such as census boundaries, state liquor-license registries, and municipal inspection records. Public records lag behind real life, so a closed bar can linger and a new one can be absent. Use Add a bar on the home screen to add one, or report a listing from the bar's page and tell us what is wrong. One report per bar per person is enough; it goes to a person to check.

What happens to photos I add, and how do I report or block someone?

Every image is re-encoded before it is saved, so the data a camera writes into the file, including GPS coordinates, device identifiers, and capture time, does not survive. Only the pixels do. Photos from app users are also reviewed by a person before they appear on a bar's page. You can report a photo, rating, message, post, bar, person, or ad, and you can block someone, which ends the friendship and stops messages and friend requests in both directions.

Notifications and the ads you see.

Posts and specials near you has its own switch in Settings, so you can turn promotional notifications off and still get messages and friend requests. Everything else is the single on-or-off switch your phone gives the app, in your phone's own settings. Ads are aimed using your resolved area only, never your exact location and never a profile of you, and there is a daily limit on how many times one campaign can reach you: three a day by default, and between one and ten if the bar sets it. You can report an ad the same way you report anything else.

I own a bar. How do I claim the listing?

Start at portal.gobarcrawl.com and claim the bar there. Claims are checked by a person before access is granted, and we may call the number you give to confirm it, so add a number we can reach you on. Once you have access you can add photos on any tier. The paid tier adds the menu, a live camera, surveys, guest messaging, and your own posts, and billing is managed in the portal.

FHShot

Quick answers for screenshots from the menu bar.

FHShot puts screen capture one click away in the Mac menu bar and saves each shot as a PNG in a folder you choose. Most questions are about permission, the save folder, and keyboard shortcuts.

My screenshots are blank. What is wrong?

macOS needs Screen Recording permission before any app can capture the screen. Without it, the capture still saves, but the image comes out blank, black, or showing only your wallpaper. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, then Screen Recording, turn FHShot on, and relaunch the app. FHShot also shows a warning row in its menu when the permission is missing, and clicking that row takes you there.

Why does it ask me to pick a folder?

FHShot runs in the macOS App Sandbox, which the Mac App Store requires, so it cannot write anywhere until you grant it a folder. It asks on your first capture, and then remembers that choice, so you only do it once. Open Save Folder and Choose Save Folder are both in the menu if you want to look or move it later.

How do keyboard shortcuts work?

No shortcut is set when you install FHShot; you assign the ones you want under Keyboard Shortcuts in the menu. A shortcut has to include Command, Control, or Option, and Shift on its own is refused. While recording one, Delete clears it and Escape leaves it as it was. If macOS already owns the combination you pick, the shortcut will simply never fire, so choose another. The greyed-out shortcuts shown next to each capture mode are macOS's own screenshot shortcuts, not FHShot's.

Can it copy to the clipboard, or wait before capturing?

Both are switches in the menu and both start off. Also Copy to Clipboard puts each shot on the clipboard as well as saving the file, so you never get a copy instead of a file. The 5-Second Delay applies to full-screen captures only, because selection and window captures wait for you anyway and macOS ignores a delay for them.

What is the row of recent captures for?

FHShot keeps the last five captures in the menu as thumbnails. Click one to open it, or drag one straight into an email, document, or chat, which hands over the real file. You can also reveal one in the Finder. The row is hidden until you have taken a shot, and an entry drops off if you move, rename, or delete the file behind it.

Where is the icon, and can it start with my Mac?

FHShot is a camera icon in the menu bar, with no Dock icon and no window. An app cannot choose where its icon sits, but you can: hold Command and drag the icon along the menu bar, and macOS keeps it there. Open at Login is a switch in the menu. macOS sometimes wants to approve that itself, in which case FHShot points you to Login Items in System Settings.

What does it need, and what does it send?

FHShot needs macOS 13 Ventura or later. It has no account, collects no analytics, and has no network permission at all, so screenshots cannot leave your Mac. If a save fails, it tells you with an alert rather than failing quietly. Cancelling a selection capture with Escape saves nothing, which is deliberate rather than an error.

FHDarts

Quick answers for scoring, boards, and stats.

FHDarts reads throws from GRANBOARD Bluetooth dartboards, and it also scores games by hand when there is no board. Most questions are about connecting, fixing a misread dart, and where the stats live.

How do I connect to my board?

Switch the board on, keep it close by, and let FHDarts scan. There is no pairing step to work through and no GRAN account to create. The first run asks how you want to score, and you can change that later in Settings. A scan gives up after 30 seconds and says no board was found; to try again, open Settings, find the Board section, and tap Scan for board. FHDarts also looks again by itself when the board powers on, after a dropped connection, and when you come back to the app. If it says the board has forgotten this device, remove it in your iPhone's Bluetooth settings and tap Scan once more. FHDarts is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by GRAN DARTS.

A dart bounced out, or the board read it wrong.

Tap Keypad and enter the dart by hand. The pad stays open for the rest of the turn, and Double and Treble apply to the next dart only. If a score has already landed wrong, Undo takes back the last dart, and it reaches back across turns and legs rather than only within the current turn.

Can I play without a board?

Yes. In Settings, under Board, set Score with to By hand. Every dart is then entered on the keypad and Bluetooth is not used at all, so the games, drills, and stats all work the same way.

Which games and drills are included?

Match play covers 301, 501, 701, and 901, each with Double in and Double out switches, Cricket with an optional Cut-throat mode, and Killer. The drills are Count-Up, Bob's 27, Around the Clock, Shanghai, Catch 40, 121 Checkout, and Halve-It, and each one keeps your personal best.

Can I play alone, and how do legs and sets work?

There is a computer opponent at five levels: Novice, Pub, League, County, and Pro. Handicaps are separate, and give an individual player a different starting score so an uneven pair still get a fair game. You can set first to a number of legs, and switch on sets as well, with up to eight players in a game. An unfinished match can be picked up again from the Play screen or from History.

How do I turn the spoken caller off?

In Settings, under Caller, switch off Call the scores. Call the remaining score and Tick on every dart are separate switches in the same place. The caller uses the speech built into iOS, so it plays sound and never listens; FHDarts does not use the microphone. If the voice sounds flat, iOS can download a better one under Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices.

Where are my stats, and do they move to another device?

Stats are built from every dart stored, and the Stats screen includes a heat map of where your darts land, with a picker for each player. Settings has a Data section that exports all matches as a CSV or a JSON file, one row per dart. History syncs through your own private iCloud account, which we cannot see or reach. Signed out of iCloud, everything still works and nothing is lost: it simply stays on that device. Sync runs while the app is open, so a second device may need opening before it catches up. FHDarts needs iOS 17 or later and runs on iPhone and iPad.

FHWordGuess

Quick answers for the five-letter word game.

FHWordGuess is a five-letter word puzzle for iPhone and iPad with no daily wait. There is also a separate support page for the app.

How do I play?

Guess the word in six tries. Each guess has to be a real five-letter word. After every guess, each tile tells you whether that letter is in the right spot, in the word but somewhere else, or not in the word at all.

I'm colour-blind. Can I still read the tiles?

Yes, and there is nothing to switch on. Every tile carries a shape as well as a colour: a checkmark for the right spot, a triangle for a letter that is in the word but in the wrong place, and a dash for a letter that is not in the word. The result never depends on colour alone. Settings explains this under Accessibility.

Do I have to wait for a new puzzle?

No. There is no once-a-day puzzle and no timer. When a round ends, tap New Game and play again as often as you like. Each round picks a fresh word and avoids the ones you have had recently.

Where is Hard Mode?

Open Settings with the gear icon on the game screen, and switch on Hard Mode under Gameplay. It makes every later guess use the hints you have already uncovered. It takes effect on your next new game rather than part-way through the one you are playing.

Can I change how it looks?

Settings has two separate controls. Appearance, under Theme, follows your system setting or stays on Light or Dark. Style, under Game Background, is either None or Gradient, and choosing Gradient lets you pick between Steel Dusk, Amber Glow, Ink Fade, and Paper Mist. The background shows behind the board on the game screen only.

Where are my statistics kept?

Games played, win rate, current and best streaks, and your guess distribution are stored on the device and sync through your own iCloud account, which we cannot see or reach. With iCloud off, or signed out, the game works exactly the same and the stats simply stay on that device. Sync runs while the app is open, so a second device may need opening before it catches up. FHWordGuess has no account of ours, no ads, and no third-party tracking, and it needs iOS 17 or later on iPhone or iPad.

What are the leaderboards and achievements?

There are two Game Center leaderboards, one for your streak and one for your wins, plus seven achievements. They are optional and they use your own Game Center account, which is Apple's service rather than ours. If you are not signed in to Game Center, the game plays exactly the same and simply posts nothing.

FHWebCheck

Quick answers for the current app.

FHWebCheck is built to keep website monitoring readable and actionable, with a clean overview screen, direct follow-up actions, organized settings, and detailed status context when users need to investigate further.

What does FHWebCheck help users see?

FHWebCheck gives users a monitoring view that highlights the sites they care about, the latest check timing, and whether each site is currently responding normally or needs attention.

What actions are available from the list?

From the site menu, users can open details, run a fresh check, review site history, edit the entry, copy the URL, open the site in Safari, or remove a site that no longer needs monitoring.

What can be controlled in settings?

The settings screen is designed to keep monitoring organized, including grouping options and monitoring behavior notes that explain how background refresh works on iPhone in real-world conditions.

What appears in website details?

The detail screen shows the current status, last check time, freshness, monitoring mode, notification preference, and the exact message returned by the latest result so users can understand the status before deciding what to do next.

Why is a site showing offline?

Temporary hosting issues, DNS problems, SSL errors, or short outages can all affect availability checks. If the issue appears briefly, try checking again after a short delay to confirm whether it was temporary.

How often are checks performed?

FHWebCheck performs checks when the app is opened and may run background activity according to iOS behavior. Background timing is not fully controlled by the app itself, so checks may not run on a fixed schedule in every situation.

Not receiving notifications?

Verify notification permissions in iPhone Settings and make sure Focus modes, summaries, or other notification controls are not delaying or suppressing alerts.

What should be included in a support message?

The most helpful messages include the monitored URL, what status you saw, when it happened, whether the issue was temporary or repeatable, and any screenshots that help explain the problem clearly.

FHCams

Support for a cleaner camera experience across mixed hardware.

FHCams is designed to make older and newer camera systems feel more polished, more commercial, and easier to scan in real time, even when properties and device types vary.

What makes FHCams different?

FHCams is built to work with a wide range of camera makes, models, and ages while presenting them through a more unified, modern interface. The goal is to help users move from fragmented feeds and dated viewing tools to one cleaner experience that feels current, practical, and easier to trust.

What can users manage?

Users can organize cameras by property, review multiple locations from one overview, adjust refresh behavior, and keep camera lists readable as installations grow.

What should be included in an FHCams support request?

Include the property name, camera name, camera brand if known, the device you were using, and whether the issue involved loading, refresh timing, live view behavior, or interface presentation. Screenshots and timing details are especially helpful when the issue is intermittent.

FHNetworkMap

Support for a network map that keeps device visibility practical.

FHNetworkMap is designed to help users see the structure of their local network, search devices faster, tune alerts, and keep the map understandable as equipment changes.

What does FHNetworkMap help users do?

FHNetworkMap gives users a visual network map, a searchable device list, practical alert settings, and an editing surface for organizing names, groups, and device details. The goal is to make local network visibility feel clearer and more useful without requiring enterprise-style tooling.

Why is a device missing or showing offline?

Devices may appear offline or may not show up if they are asleep, disconnected from the current network, filtered by the current view, blocked from discovery, or were not reachable during a recent scan. If the result looks temporary, run another scan and compare the seen timing before assuming the device is truly unavailable.

What can be adjusted in settings?

Users can tune auto-scan behavior, timeout values, notification preferences, offline thresholds, and default port-scan settings. Export tools are also available so the current map can be shared or reviewed outside the app when needed.

What can be edited for each device?

FHNetworkMap supports renaming devices, assigning groups, changing icons, configuring alert behavior, and enabling actions like Wake-on-LAN where the target device supports it. This helps the network map stay accurate as equipment moves, changes, or gets repurposed.

What should be included in a support message?

The most helpful support requests include the device name, local IP address if relevant, what screen you were using, what behavior looked wrong, whether the issue is repeatable, and screenshots or exported data if you are comfortable sharing them.
